## XC

I made some of my best friends on the cross country team, and pushed past barriers that I thought were never possible before the season. From beginning 9th grade with a 17 minute 2-mile at tryouts for soccer, I progressed to a 21 minute 5k and 12:55 2 mile by the end of the cross country season. Competing at races, and supporting my teammates as we ran through grueling heat and a seemingly endless distance formed some of my strongest bonds in high school.

## Running
